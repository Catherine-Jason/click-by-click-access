# Click by Click Access

Free onboarding, offboarding and device inventory, one step at a time.

This grew out of my Cyber Ready Clinic capstone, [Click by Click Offboarding](https://github.com/Catherine-Jason/click-by-click-offboarding). That app walks you through closing every account and collecting every device when someone leaves. Click by Click Access starts from the same app and adds onboarding, so access is set up right from the first day.

The Onboarding tab builds a checklist for someone who is starting, with their own accounts, MFA, devices that are ready to use and a short security talk, and gives you a dated record of the access you granted.

The Inventory tab keeps track of every device your organization owns, shows which ones need updates, are missing or have a warranty ending soon, and updates itself when you check in a device from someone who is leaving.

It is free and open source under the MIT License. Everything runs in the browser. Nothing gets saved to a server, so download a backup now and then to keep your data safe.

**Built with:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
